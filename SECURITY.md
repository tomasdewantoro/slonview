# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities by email to **security@slonview.com**, not in a public issue. You'll get a reply within 7 days.

Include the Slonview version, your OS, steps to reproduce, and the impact. Scope and known limits (read-only mode is a guard, not a permission system; `prefer`/`require` TLS does not verify certificates) are on <https://slonview.com/security>.
