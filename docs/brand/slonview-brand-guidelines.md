## 1. The idea

The mark is a closed window frame with one highlighted row passing through a notch in its right wall. Two quiet rows stay inside the frame.

- **Small app, whole table** (internal design rationale, not public copy). The frame is small and the row is longer than the frame. This is the brand's speed and size lead in one shape. Public copy may say "whole table" only about export until the WKWebView scrolling check passes (strategy §12).
- **It is the paging mechanism.** Slonview pages through a fixed window, 500 rows at a time. The row shown in transit is the page moving through the view.
- **Motion comes from offset and crop.** There are no speed lines, swooshes or blur.
- **Red stays free.** The accent is plum on light grounds and lilac on dark grounds. Red is used only for the WRITE badge and for errors.

## 3. Logo system

### 3.1 Files

| Asset | Colourways | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol (64-unit master) | light, dark, mono, reversed | `identity/symbol/symbol-<mode>.svg`, `@1x.png` (128 px), `@2x.png` (256 px) |

| Wordmark (outlined) | light, dark, mono, reversed | `identity/wordmark/wordmark-<mode>.svg`, `@1x.png`, `@2x.png` |
| Wordmark (editable text) | light | `identity/wordmark/wordmark-editable.svg` (layout drafts only; needs `../fonts/`) |

Each colourway has a ground it is meant for:
- **light:** Paper `#F4F5F8` or white;
- **dark:** Graphite `#1B1D21` or `#212328`;
- **mono:** any light ground, in one colour (ink);
- **reversed:** plum `#6E2366`, ink, or a photograph darker than L* 35, in white.

Every outlined SVG is free of `<text>`, `<image>`, `data:image`, `font-family` and filters. The build script checks this on every run.

### 3.2 Construction

- **Symbol (64 grid):**
  - Frame: 44 × 40 at (4, 12), corner radius 9, wall 5.5.
  - Row: 8 tall at y 28, running from x 13 to x 61, with fully rounded ends.
  - Notch: row height + 1.6 above and below.
  - Quiet rows: 24 × 3.6 at y 20.6 and y 39.8.
- **Wordmark:**
  - Hanken Grotesk at wght 640 with −0.012 em tracking, outlined.
  - The dot of the "i" is replaced by a short plum row: 2.1 × stem width, 0.92 × stem height, starting at the stem's left edge.
  - This keeps "Slonview" one unbroken word. There is no gap after "Slo" (strategy §8.2).
- **Lockup:**
  - Symbol height = 1.55 × the wordmark's cap height.
  - Gap between symbol and wordmark = 0.42 × symbol height.
  - The two are optically centred on the cap height.

### 3.3 Clearspace

The unit **R** is the height of the accent row in the symbol (8/64 of the symbol height).

- Keep at least **3R** clear on all sides of the symbol and of the lockup. Measure from the frame on the left, top and bottom, and from the row's tip on the right.
- On the wordmark alone, keep **1R** at the wordmark's own scale (the height of the i-dot row × 1.1) on all sides.
- The row's right tip is part of the mark. Never crop it, and never let another element touch it.

### 3.5 Misuse

<!-- fig:misuse -->

The sheet above (`identity/checks/misuse.png`) shows eight real misuses of the mark:

1. **Red row.** Red belongs to WRITE and errors.
2. **Old UI blue** (or any other new accent).
3. **Squashing or stretching.**
4. **Rotating.**
5. **Opening the right wall.** This brings back the logout reading MAJOR-2 removed.
6. **Adding speed lines,** motion blur or swooshes.
7. **Baking in a drop shadow or glow.** Platforms add their own.
8. **Placing it on a ground below 3:1** for any part. Light colourway on graphite is one example.

Also don't:
- put the mark next to, or inside, a PostgreSQL or Slonik mark (strategy §6.4);
- set "Slonview" in another font in place of the outlined wordmark;
- recolour the quiet rows to the accent (they would then read as a hamburger);
- animate the mark inside the app (the app has no splash screen).

**macOS tinted and clear appearances (macOS 26 Icon Composer).** Not delivered as a `.icon` bundle, because Icon Composer is a GUI app. To build one:
1. Import `layer-background-light.svg` as the background group and `layer-foreground-tint-greyscale.svg` as the foreground group. The foreground is white frame, `#E6E6E6` row and `#B3B3B3` quiet rows, so macOS's tint keeps the row-versus-frame hierarchy.
2. For Clear, use `layer-foreground-white.svg` with the system's default glass. The notch and the row's overhang still read, because they are shape, not colour.
3. Don't add specular highlights or shadows. Icon Composer adds them.

### 4.3 Install in the app

Installed in `src-tauri/icons/` by `docs/brand/identity/build/install_icons.sh`:
1. `pnpm tauri icon docs/brand/identity/appicon/icon-light-1024.png` generates the desktop set. The `android/` and `ios/` outputs are deleted, because Slonview is desktop only.
2. `tauri icon` only downscales, so the script then rebuilds `icon.icns` with `iconutil` and `icon.ico` with Pillow (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 256), using the hand-tuned 16 and 32 px masters. It also replaces `32x32.png`.

Verified by extracting the installed `icon.icns`: its `icon_16x16.png` is pixel-identical to the hand-tuned `icon-light-16.png`. `tauri.conf.json` is unchanged; it already points at `32x32.png`, `128x128.png`, `128x128@2x.png`, `icon.icns` and `icon.ico`.

**What the 16 px icon reads as now:**
- A closed square frame with three bars. The middle bar is lilac and runs through the frame's right wall.
- **Logout and exit:** these glyphs are an open bracket with an arrowhead. Slonview has no arrowhead, its frame is closed on all four sides apart from the notch, and its row is a bar with two bars beside it.
- **C, G, €:** the right wall above and below the row is now 3 px tall at 16 px, so the frame no longer reads as an open curve.
- **Battery:** the battery's nub is short, centred and outside a hollow box. Slonview's row starts inside the frame and extends 3 px out, and two quiet rows sit inside.
- **Hamburger:** the quiet rows are shorter and lighter than the row, and the frame is closed.
- **Scrollbar and progress bar:** the bar is horizontal and crosses the wall, which neither control does.

  1. On a selection, secondary text (`faint`, `null-fg`, `warn` / PK) switches to `selection-fg-muted`.
  2. On accent fills, text uses `accent-fg`; secondary text uses `accent-fg-muted`.

## 12. Remaining risks

1. **No FigCraft master** (review MINOR-7, QA MINOR-A). GUI apps were out of scope for this run. The layered SVGs are the editable source; the owner should accept this at Gate 3 or schedule a FigCraft import.
2. **No macOS 26 `.icon` bundle.** Tinted and clear appearances are specified (§4.2) and the layers are supplied, but nothing was rendered in Icon Composer.
3. **Bundling fonts in the app** adds about 320 KB. Re-check "about 8 MB on disk" on the signed build (strategy §9).
4. **Lilac is 37° from indigo by hue.** It is distinct today (ΔE 45). Hold the hue at 272° or more.
5. **Editor numbers and error red under deuteranopia.** Amber `#E0B45C` vs error dark is ΔE 50.1, but only 29.9 under deuteranopia. The error mark is a wavy underline plus a tinted background, never a text colour, so the two don't compete for the same role.
6. **Tokens are not wired into the app yet.** `src/index.css`, `ResultGrid.tsx` and `SqlEditor.tsx` still use the old colours until the UI/UX stage applies `tokens.css` and §11.2.
